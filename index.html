Privacy Policy

Fetch with Luna · Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fetch with Luna (“the App,” “we,” or “us”) handles information when you play it. Fetch with Luna is a simple game of fetch with a dog named Luna. It has no accounts and no server of our own — most of what follows is about the advertising technology we use to keep the game free, not about us collecting your data directly.

1. Information we do not collect

Fetch with Luna does not require you to create an account, and we do not operate any backend server that stores your personal information. Specifically, we do not collect your name, email address, or any other directly-identifying information through the App.

2. Information stored on your device

The App stores one small piece of data locally on your device, using standard on-device storage: whether you've completed the first-time walkthrough, so we don't show it to you again. This information never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us. Uninstalling the App removes it.

3. Advertising

Fetch with Luna displays advertisements, served through Google AdMob (Google's mobile advertising service), to support the free game. This is the main way information about your device or usage leaves the App.

What this involves

When an ad is requested, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process information such as:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (e.g. Android's Advertising ID) and other app- or device-level identifiers used for advertising and fraud prevention
  • General device information (device type, operating system, language, screen size)
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interaction data (such as which ads were shown or tapped), used for ad delivery, frequency capping, and measurement
  • Diagnostic information about how the ads software is performing

This information is collected and processed by Google and its advertising partners under their own privacy policies, not by us directly. You can read more about how Google handles this information here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • How AdMob handles personal data

Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region that requires it, you may be shown a consent form from Google when you open the App, asking how your data may be used for advertising. You can change your choice at any time where the App offers that option, or by clearing the App's data so the form is shown again.

Your choices for ad personalization

  • Android: Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads → “Opt out of Ads Personalization” (exact wording varies by Android version). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID from the same screen.

Opting out reduces ad personalization but does not stop ads from being shown, since that's how the free game is funded.

4. Children's privacy

Fetch with Luna is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

5. Data security

Because the App doesn't operate its own servers or user accounts, there's no central database of your personal information for us to secure. Data stored locally on your device is protected by your device's own operating-system security.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example as the game's features or advertising setup change. We'll update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page when we do. Continuing to play after a change means you accept the updated policy.
