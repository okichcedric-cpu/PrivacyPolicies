Privacy Policy

Luna: The Pomodoro Dog · Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Luna: The Pomodoro Dog (“the App,” “we,” or “us”) handles information when you use it. We built Luna to be a simple focus-timer app with no accounts and no server of our own — most of what follows is about the advertising technology we use to keep the App free, not about us collecting your data directly.

1. Information we do not collect

Luna does not require you to create an account, and we do not operate any backend server that stores your personal information. Specifically, we do not collect your name, email address, or any other directly-identifying information through the App.

2. Information stored on your device

The App stores a small amount of data locally on your device, using standard on-device storage. This information never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us:

  • Your chosen focus-session length (so it's remembered the next time you open the App)
  • Whether you've completed the first-time onboarding walkthrough
  • The scheduled end time of a session currently in progress (so the App can resume correctly if it's closed and reopened)

Uninstalling the App removes all of this data.

3. Notifications

The App schedules a local notification on your device to let you know when a focus session has finished, in case your screen is locked or the App is in the background. This is handled entirely by your device's operating system — it is not a push notification, and no notification data is sent to us or to any server.

4. Advertising

Luna displays advertisements, served through Google AdMob (Google's mobile advertising service), to support the free version of the App. This is the main way information about your device or usage leaves the App.

What this involves

When an ad is requested, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process information such as:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (e.g. Android's Advertising ID)
  • General device information (device type, operating system, language, screen size)
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interaction data (such as which ads were shown or tapped), used for ad delivery, frequency capping, and measurement

This information is collected and processed by Google and its advertising partners under their own privacy policies, not by us directly. You can read more about how Google handles this information here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • How AdMob handles personal data

Your choices for ad personalization

  • Android: Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization" (exact wording varies by Android version)

Opting out reduces ad personalization but does not stop ads from being shown, since that's how the free version of the App is funded.

5. Children's privacy

Luna is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

6. Data security

Because the App doesn't operate its own servers or user accounts, there's no central database of your personal information for us to secure. Data stored locally on your device is protected by your device's own operating-system security.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example as the App's features or advertising setup change. We'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page when we do. Continuing to use the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
